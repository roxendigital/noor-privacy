Noor Privacy Policy

Effective 1 November 2026

Noor shows prayer times, the Qibla direction, a Hijri calendar and dua. It works everything out on your phone. The developer cannot see your location or what you do in Noor, because Noor has no account system, no server of its own and no analytics, and it does not show ads.

What Noor stores

Everything Noor keeps is saved on your phone:

  • Your place: the city name and its coordinates, whether you chose it or Noor found it from your location. Cities you add to the Prayers card are saved the same way.
  • Your prayer settings: calculation method, Asr rule, minute offsets, Hijri date adjustment, alert choices and reminders for each prayer.
  • Your activity: tasbih counts, the days you mark as fasted in the Ramadan tracker, and your favourite and pinned dua.
  • App settings: theme, text size, 12 or 24 hour time, and whether Pro is unlocked.
  • A short-lived copy of recent city searches, kept for 24 hours so repeat searches are quick.

Your location

Noor uses your location only while the app is open, and only to work out prayer times and the Qibla direction. It never tracks you in the background, and the developer never receives your coordinates.

Prayer times, the Qibla bearing and the Hijri date are calculated on your phone from your coordinates, the date and the method you choose. They work with no internet connection.

When you choose Use my location, Noor also asks your phone's built-in location service to turn your coordinates into a city name. On Android that service is provided by Google, and on iPhone it is provided by Apple. They receive your coordinates for that request and handle them under their own terms. Noor receives only the name. If you would rather not do this, choose a city by name instead.

What leaves your phone

Your settings, activity and history never leave your phone unless you send them. The only things Noor sends over the internet are described here.

  • Typed city searches. When you type a city name, that text is sent to OpenStreetMap Nominatim, a free map search service, to find the city's coordinates. Like any online request, it also shows them your IP address. Nothing else is sent, and your own coordinates are not part of the request. They handle it under the OpenStreetMap privacy policy.
  • City names from your location. See Your location above.
  • Sharing and copying a dua. When you tap Copy, the dua text is placed on your phone's clipboard. When you tap Share, it opens your phone's share sheet. The text goes wherever you send it.
  • Support email. If you choose Support, your email app opens with a message to the address below. Noor adds nothing except the subject line.
  • The Pro purchase. See Purchases below.

Noor has no ads, no analytics and no crash-reporting service. It bundles its photos, fonts and dua inside the app, so it downloads none of them.

Notifications and permissions

Noor asks for permission to show notifications so it can alert you at prayer time. Alerts are created on your phone and need no internet connection. There is no push server, and Noor sends no promotional notifications.

  • Location, while using the app. Used as described above. You can refuse it and choose a city instead.
  • Exact alarms (Android). Lets alerts arrive on time. Noor also re-arms its alerts after your phone restarts, and may vibrate for an alert if you choose that.
  • Motion sensors. The compass on the Qibla screen reads your phone's magnetic sensor. This needs no permission, and the readings stay on your phone.

Noor does not ask for your contacts, camera, microphone or photos, and it does not use background location.

Phone backups

Your phone's own backup system may copy Noor's data. On Android that is your Google backup, and on iPhone it is iCloud, if you have them turned on. Those copies are stored by Google or Apple under their own terms, and you control them in your phone's settings. Noor does not read or manage them.

Purchases

Noor Pro is a one-time purchase made through Google Play or the Apple App Store. It is not a subscription. The store handles the payment and keeps its own records under its own terms. Noor does not see your card or payment details.

To unlock Pro, and to restore it if you reinstall or change phones, Noor uses a service called RevenueCat that checks purchases. When you buy or restore Pro, and when Noor checks whether Pro is unlocked, the app contacts RevenueCat. RevenueCat receives a random identifier made for your install, details of your purchase (what was bought and when), and the technical information any online service sees, such as your IP address and device type. RevenueCat does not receive your location, your settings or anything else you do in Noor. It handles this information under its own privacy policy.

The app keeps a record on your phone of whether Pro is unlocked.

This website

The Noor website has a live prayer-times demo. It works out the times in your browser, using the same method as the app. If you press Use my location, your browser asks your permission, and the coordinates stay in your browser and are never sent anywhere. The demo remembers your last city and method in your browser's local storage. The website uses no cookies and no analytics. It is hosted on GitHub Pages, and GitHub may keep standard server logs, such as visitors' IP addresses, under its own privacy statement. The Tell me when it is out button opens your email app and sends nothing by itself.

Who Noor is for

Noor is a general prayer app for anyone. It is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect information from them. Because Noor does not collect personal information at all, there is no child data to collect.

Keeping and deleting your data

Your data stays on your phone until you remove it. You can erase everything with Erase all data in Settings, or by uninstalling Noor. Phone backups you made are separate copies, so delete those as well if you want everything gone. The developer holds no copy of your data, so there is nothing to ask the developer to delete. Purchase records are held by the app store and RevenueCat. To ask for those to be deleted, contact us at the address below and we will pass your request on.

Changes to this policy

If Noor changes how it handles information, this page will be updated first and the effective date above will change. Noor does not plan to add accounts, ads or analytics.
